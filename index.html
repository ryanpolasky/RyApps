RyHome

Your Matter smart home, on your PC.

RyHome runs its own Matter controller right inside the app, so it talks to your devices directly over your network. No hub, no Mac, no cloud account. It works alongside Apple Home, Google Home, or Alexa instead of replacing them.

How it works

Your PC RyHome with its built-in Matter engine
your Wi-Fi or Ethernet
Matter devices Lights, plugs, locks, blinds, fans, thermostats, sensors
also paired with
Your other home apps Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa keep working
  • Lights, properly Brightness, a color wheel, white temperature, and swatches you can overwrite with your own colors.
  • Groups and scenes Turn several bulbs into one tile, and save scenes from how your lights look right now.
  • More than lights Plugs, switches, locks, blinds, fans, thermostats, and temperature, humidity, motion, contact, and leak sensors.
  • Lives in the tray All Lights Off and your scenes are a right-click away, and it can start with Windows.
  • Backups Export your pairings, rooms, groups, and scenes to one file and restore them on a new PC.

Before you install

  • Your PC needs to be on the same network as your devices.
  • Add a device with the pairing code from the app it's already set up in. In Apple Home that's the accessory's settings, then Turn On Pairing Mode. RyHome can't do Bluetooth setup, so brand new devices need setting up there first.
  • Windows Firewall asks about Node.js the first time. That's RyHome's engine, so allow it on private networks.
  • It isn't code signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run. Click More info, then Run anyway.

RyMessage

Your texts, on Windows.

Your Mac stays the real iMessage device. A small server on it hands your conversations to the Windows app, which reads and sends them like a native messaging app, with notifications that pop up even when the window is closed.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

How it works

Your PC The RyMessage app
home network or Tailscale
Your Mac The RyMessage server, running at login
reads and sends through
Messages Your iMessage and SMS conversations
  • Notifications that float New texts slide in at the top of your screen, even with the window closed to the tray.
  • The good stuff Reactions, threaded replies, live edits and unsends, jumbo emoji, and :shortcode: autocomplete.
  • Photos, videos, files Drag them in, paste them, or pick them, and voice and video messages play inline.
  • Your own sounds Upload a notification sound, then trim it and fade it right in the app.

Before you install

  • You need a Mac that stays on and signed in to Messages. Run the server's install script there and it prints a pairing code for the Windows app.
  • The PC and Mac need to reach each other. A private network like Tailscale works great.
  • It isn't code signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first run. Click More info, then Run anyway.
